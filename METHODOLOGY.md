# Research Methodology

## Study Area and Sampling Design

The research was conducted across 34 freshwater sampling sites distributed among 11 protected areas within the RomaNatura system in Rome, Italy.

The study focused on aquatic insect biodiversity in freshwater habitats, including ponds and ditches.

## Field Sampling

Fieldwork involved the collection of freshwater samples for environmental DNA (eDNA) analysis.

Water samples were filtered to capture environmental DNA present in the aquatic environment. The resulting samples were subsequently processed for molecular analysis.

## Laboratory Procedures

My experimental contribution included:

- Water filtration.
- Environmental DNA extraction.
- PCR amplification.

The research used eDNA metabarcoding approaches targeting the COI and 16S mitochondrial markers to investigate aquatic insect diversity.

## Biodiversity Assessment

The resulting metabarcoding data were used within the research project to investigate taxonomic richness, community composition and ecological differences among freshwater habitats.

Statistical outputs were produced within the project collaboration. My contribution focused on interpreting these results in relation to the ecological questions addressed by the thesis.

## Research Scope

The study examined aquatic insect biodiversity across protected freshwater habitats and explored the influence of habitat characteristics and spatial patterns on biodiversity.

This document provides a general overview of the research workflow. Detailed project protocols and underlying research data are not included.
